              Developer

              Limits

              • Not a medical device. A state is an estimate from your face and posture, a pattern, not a diagnosis. If tiredness keeps coming back, talk to a health professional.
              • Glasses, lighting or a new seating position make readings less accurate. Recalibrate above.
              • Holding a phone at chest height can sometimes read as "Mildly tired". Hold it at chin height or in your lap.
              • Equilibre only reads tiredness. It does not guess emotions.
