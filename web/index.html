The problem

Tired isn’t a mood. It’s a signal.

Tiredness builds over hours at the screen, and you rarely notice it while it builds. It does show in your face: longer blinks, eyes closed for longer, more yawns. Break reminders (Pomodoro) run on a fixed schedule. Equilibre watches for these signs and shows you when they appear.

01 How it works

From your normal to a nudge.

  1. Step 1

    Calibrate

    Close your eyes for 3 seconds, then work as usual for 5 minutes while Equilibre learns your normal.

  2. 478 points

    Step 2

    Watch, on your laptop

    MediaPipe reads 478 face points about 10 times a second, inside your browser.

  3. Tired

    Eyes closed 18% of the last minute

    Step 3

    See the pattern

    Every 10 seconds you get a state: Normal, Mildly tired, Tired, Asleep or Away, with the reason behind it.

  4. Advice

    Take a brisk walk or climb a few flights of stairs.

03 Privacy

Chill out. Your face never leaves your laptop.

  • 01

    Video is never sent

    The camera is read inside your browser tab. No video or image of your face leaves it.

  • 02

    History stays on your laptop

    Your states and calibration are kept in this browser for 30 days.

  • 03

    Advice sends numbers only

    When you ask for advice, only your state and a few numbers are sent.

  • 04

    We keep your name and email

    Supabase stores them to sign you in, with sign-in times, IP address and browser.

Not a medical device. Equilibre shows a pattern of tiredness and cannot diagnose anything. If tiredness keeps coming back, talk to a health professional.

Know your pattern before the 3 p.m. crash.
