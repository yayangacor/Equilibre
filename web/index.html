Wellness and productivity

You can’t see what your body feels.

Equilibre reads the early signs of tiredness from your webcam — eye closure, slow blinks, yawns, head position — and shows you the pattern before the crash. It tells a pattern, not a diagnosis.

The problem

Tired isn’t a mood. It’s a signal.

Tiredness builds from short sleep and long, unbroken stretches at the screen, and you rarely notice it while it builds. A timer can’t see it: Pomodoro rings every 25 minutes whether you’re sharp or fading. It doesn’t adapt to how your body feels.

01 — How it works

From your normal to a nudge.

  1. Step 1

    Calibrate

    Close your eyes for 3 seconds, then work as usual for 5 minutes while Equilibre learns your normal.

  2. 478 points

    Step 2

    Watch, on your laptop

    MediaPipe reads 478 face points about 10 times a second, inside your browser.

  3. Tired

    Eyes closed 18% of the last minute

    Step 3

    See the pattern

    Every 10 seconds you get a state: Normal, Mildly tired, Tired, Asleep or Away, with the reason behind it.

  4. Nudge

    Take a short walk.

    Ask for advice →

    Step 4

    Act on it

    Sit upright, take a short walk, get some daylight. Ask for advice and an AI flow on IBM Langflow answers with sources (WHO, NIOSH and Indonesian occupational-health guidance).

02 — What you see vs. what we see

Your face becomes a few numbers.

The video stays in your browser tab. Only these numbers are kept, on this device.

03 — Privacy

Chill out. Your face never leaves your laptop.

Never sent
Video and images of your face.
Only on this device
Your fatigue history and calibration (30 days).
Stored by us
Your name and email, to sign you in (Supabase). Supabase also logs sign-in times, IP address and browser.

When you ask for advice, only your state and a few numbers are sent — never your face.

Not a medical device. Equilibre shows a pattern, not a diagnosis. If tiredness keeps coming back, talk to a health professional.

Know your pattern before the 3 p.m. crash.
